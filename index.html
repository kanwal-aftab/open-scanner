Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how OpenScanner accesses, processes, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Process

OpenScanner allows users to scan documents, ID cards, and books, convert images into PDFs, and extract text using OCR.

Documents and images selected or captured by users are processed to provide the requested scanning functionality.

2. Camera Permission

The application requests camera access to capture documents and images. Camera access is used only for scanning features.

3. Photos and File Access

The application may access images and files selected by users to import, enhance, convert, or save documents.

4. Document Storage

Scanned documents, images, and generated PDFs are stored locally on the user's device. We do not upload these files to our servers.

5. OCR and Text Extraction

OCR functionality extracts text from scanned documents. OCR processing takes place locally on the device, and extracted text is not transmitted to our servers.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share scanned documents, extracted text, or personal information with third parties. Users may choose to share exported documents using other applications.

7. Data Security

We aim to protect user information by limiting access to the permissions required for app functionality. Users should take appropriate precautions to secure their own devices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Documents stored locally remain on the device until the user deletes them. Users can delete documents through the application's available document management features.

9. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Third-Party Services

If third-party services are integrated into the application, this Privacy Policy will be updated to disclose relevant data processing and sharing practices.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our application or legal obligations. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
